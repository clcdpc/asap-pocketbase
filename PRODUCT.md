# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Library staff and patrons. Staff are librarians or administrators working in well-lit professional environments, managing high volumes of material suggestions. Patrons are members of the public submitting requests from various locations, often on mobile devices, seeking a clear and reliable path to requesting library materials.

## Product Purpose
ASAP streamlines the lifecycle of patron material suggestions for public libraries. It automates catalog monitoring, hold placement, and fulfillment tracking, reducing administrative overhead and providing a faster, more transparent experience for the community.

## Brand Personality
Efficient, Professional, Easy to Use. The interface should feel like a high-performance tool that respects the user's time and expertise.

## Anti-references
Not too playful. Avoid overly round "bubbly" UI, casual illustrations, or neon "startup" aesthetics. Avoid the generic SaaS-cliché (excessive white space, light-gray-on-white text, and "magic" AI branding).

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Cleverness**: Every action should be unambiguous.
- **Institutional Trust**: The interface must feel as stable and reliable as the library itself.
- **Information Density for Experts**: Staff screens should prioritize visibility of data to support fast decision-making without feeling cluttered.
- **Mobile-First Submission**: The patron experience must be seamless on a phone, focusing on a single task at a time.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Targeting WCAG 2.1 AA. Must support high-contrast text and clear focus indicators for keyboard navigation. Considerations for older patrons who may require larger touch targets and readable font sizes.
